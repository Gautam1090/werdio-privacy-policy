Privacy Policy for Werdio

Last updated: September 18, 2026

GP codework ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Werdio mobile game ("Werdio" or the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Werdio does not require you to create an account, provide your name, phone number, or email address to play the game.

The App may store gameplay information locally on your device, such as game progress, unlocked levels, coins, rewards, and settings. This information is used to provide the game's functionality.

Werdio may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may process device and advertising information, such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, diagnostics, and ad interaction information, subject to Google's policies and your device/ad settings.

2. How We Use Information

  • Save and restore game progress and settings.
  • Provide gameplay features such as rewards and level progression.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the App.

3. Advertising

Werdio uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. AdMob may use information collected from your device to provide, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted by applicable settings and law.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information processed by third-party services, including advertising providers such as Google AdMob, may be handled by those providers according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay data stored locally by Werdio remains on your device until it is removed by the App, cleared through your device/app settings, or the App is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of the device and operating system.

Third-party service data is retained and handled according to the applicable third-party provider's policies.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Werdio is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The App does not require an account or direct submission of personal information to play.

8. Your Choices

You can manage advertising and privacy-related settings through your Android device and Google account settings where available. You can also remove locally stored gameplay data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published at this location with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Werdio's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Werdio Google Play listing.
